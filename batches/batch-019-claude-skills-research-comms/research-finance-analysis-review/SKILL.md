# Research Finance Analysis Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when reviewing financial assumptions inside research plans,
grant budgets, research operations forecasts, or unit economics evidence.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify research objective, funding source, budget owner, time horizon,
   cost categories, and source model.
2. Separate known costs, estimates, assumptions, contingencies, revenue or grant
   assumptions, and approval dependencies.
3. Check formulas, units, timing, headcount, vendor, and overhead assumptions
   against source evidence.
4. Flag financial, compliance, procurement, and approval risks for specialist
   review.
5. Produce analysis as planning support, not accounting, tax, or investment
   advice.

## Expected Output

- finance scope and source summary
- assumption and formula findings
- cost and funding risk notes
- approval and compliance gaps
- sensitivity checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- source model check
- formula and unit check
- assumption evidence check
- finance boundary check

## Failure Handling

If source numbers are missing or stale, state the gap and provide a bounded
sensitivity review.

# Business Team Communications Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when teams need to review internal updates, stakeholder messages,
escalation notes, or cross-functional coordination for clarity and alignment.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify audience, sender, decision context, sensitivity, timing, and desired
   reader action.
2. Separate facts, decisions, requests, deadlines, risks, and optional context.
3. Check that the message names owners, next steps, and escalation paths without
   overstating authority or certainty.
4. Review tone for clarity, accountability, and avoidable ambiguity.
5. Produce revisions or findings that preserve the original business intent.

## Expected Output

- audience and purpose summary
- clarity and actionability findings
- risk and sensitivity notes
- suggested message structure
- confirmation checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- audience fit check
- owner and action check
- unsupported claim check
- sensitivity and escalation check

## Failure Handling

If audience or authority is unclear, mark the message as draft and list the
approvals or facts needed before sending.

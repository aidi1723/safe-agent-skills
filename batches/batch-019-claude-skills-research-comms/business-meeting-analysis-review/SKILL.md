# Business Meeting Analysis Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a team needs to turn meeting notes, transcripts, or
summaries into reliable decisions, actions, risks, and follow-up evidence.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify meeting purpose, participants, decision owner, time horizon, and
   source artifact.
2. Separate confirmed decisions, open questions, action items, risks, blockers,
   and background discussion.
3. Check whether every action item has an owner, due date or trigger, expected
   output, and dependency notes.
4. Flag unsupported conclusions, ambiguous decisions, and missing stakeholder
   confirmation.
5. Produce a follow-up record that preserves uncertainty instead of inventing
   consensus.

## Expected Output

- meeting purpose and source summary
- decision and action register
- open questions and blockers
- owner and dependency gaps
- follow-up checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- source note check
- owner and due-date check
- unsupported decision check
- ambiguity and escalation check

## Failure Handling

If the notes are incomplete, produce a draft register and list the exact
confirmations needed from participants.

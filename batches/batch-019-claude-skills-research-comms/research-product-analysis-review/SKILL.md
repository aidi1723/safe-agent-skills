# Research Product Analysis Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when reviewing product research artifacts such as interviews,
surveys, usability notes, feature demand evidence, or roadmap research briefs.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify research question, target users, sample, method, time period, and
   decision owner.
2. Separate observed behavior, stated preference, inferred need, product
   hypothesis, and roadmap implication.
3. Check whether themes are supported by evidence volume, user fit, and
   counterexamples.
4. Flag leading questions, sample bias, weak synthesis, and overbroad product
   claims.
5. Produce research findings with confidence levels and next learning steps.

## Expected Output

- research question and method summary
- evidence-backed theme map
- bias and confidence notes
- product implication boundaries
- next research recommendations

## Verifier Expectations

- user segment check
- evidence-to-theme check
- counterexample check
- roadmap claim boundary check

## Failure Handling

If evidence is thin, recommend a learning plan instead of treating findings as
validated demand.

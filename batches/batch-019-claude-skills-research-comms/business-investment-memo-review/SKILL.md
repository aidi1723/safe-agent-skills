# Business Investment Memo Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when reviewing investment memos, business cases, diligence
summaries, or capital allocation rationale for internal decision support.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify decision owner, investment type, time horizon, capital at risk,
   source materials, and approval boundary.
2. Separate thesis, evidence, assumptions, valuation or return model, risks,
   mitigations, alternatives, and open diligence items.
3. Check whether market, finance, product, legal, and operational claims trace
   to sources and stated assumptions.
4. Flag unsupported return claims, missing downside cases, conflicts of
   interest, and specialist review needs.
5. Produce a memo quality review, not financial, legal, tax, or investment
   advice.

## Expected Output

- investment context summary
- thesis and evidence map
- assumption and downside findings
- diligence gap register
- decision-readiness checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- source and assumption check
- downside case check
- unsupported return claim check
- financial advice boundary check

## Failure Handling

If evidence or authority is incomplete, mark the memo as not decision-ready and
list the diligence needed before approval.

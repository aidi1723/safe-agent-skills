# Content Brand Voice Boundary

## When To Use

Use this skill when marketing copy, product content, help text, or executive
messaging must stay within a defined brand voice and editorial standard.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the target audience, content channel, brand voice rules, and words
   or claims that must be avoided.
2. Compare the draft against measurable voice signals: formality, sentence
   length, jargon density, certainty level, CTA style, and banned phrases.
3. Separate voice issues from factual, legal, SEO, or accessibility issues.
4. Recommend minimal edits that bring the copy back inside the voice boundary
   without changing unsupported facts.
5. Record unresolved style ambiguity when no approved brand guide exists.

## Expected Output

- voice boundary checklist
- tone drift findings
- suggested rewrite targets
- retained factual claims
- unresolved brand-guide gaps

## Verifier Expectations

- brand guide or stated voice check
- banned phrase check
- tone and certainty check
- factual claim preservation check

## Failure Handling

If no brand voice reference exists, state the assumed voice criteria before
reviewing and mark the result as advisory.

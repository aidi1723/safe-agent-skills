# Content Claims Compliance Filter

## When To Use

Use this skill before publishing or handing off content that makes product,
performance, pricing, safety, regulatory, ranking, or market claims.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the target market, channel, audience, and claim-risk category.
2. Flag absolute or unverifiable wording such as best, first, guaranteed,
   risk-free, permanent, official, certified, or exclusive unless sourced.
3. Separate factual claims from positioning, opinions, and calls to action.
4. Require source evidence for measurable, legal, medical, financial,
   technical, safety, or time-sensitive claims.
5. Suggest safer wording that preserves intent without overclaiming.

## Expected Output

- risky claim list
- unsupported or absolute wording notes
- safer wording suggestions
- required source or owner review notes
- publication readiness status

## Verifier Expectations

- claim-to-source check
- absolute language check
- market or channel assumption check
- sensitive topic escalation check

## Failure Handling

If the target jurisdiction, product facts, or source evidence is missing, mark
the review as preliminary and request owner review before publication.

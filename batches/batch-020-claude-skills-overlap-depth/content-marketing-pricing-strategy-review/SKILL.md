# Content Marketing Pricing Strategy Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when marketing, growth, product marketing, or content teams need
to review pricing narratives, package descriptions, offer framing, or
pricing-page claims.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify audience, offer, package, price claim, channel, experiment status,
   and approval owner.
2. Separate commercial pricing decisions from marketing copy, positioning,
   buyer objections, proof points, and test hypotheses.
3. Check whether price, discount, savings, ROI, and comparison claims are
   source-backed and aligned with approved commercial policy.
4. Flag legal, finance, revenue, and claims-compliance risks before publishing.
5. Produce messaging and experiment review, not final pricing approval.

## Expected Output

- pricing message context
- claim and evidence findings
- positioning and objection notes
- policy and compliance risks
- publishing readiness checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- approved price source check
- claim evidence check
- discount and savings boundary check
- commercial approval check

## Failure Handling

If approved pricing or policy evidence is missing, mark the content as not
publish-ready and list the needed approvals.

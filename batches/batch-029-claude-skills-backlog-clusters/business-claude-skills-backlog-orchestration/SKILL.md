# Business Claude Skills Backlog Orchestration

## When To Use

Use this skill when a backlog candidate concerns business operations, product
strategy, SaaS scaffolding, competitive teardown, UX research, executive
advisory templates, roadmaps, experiments, or operating-model workflows.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the business decision, audience, artifact, decision owner, time
   horizon, and available evidence.
2. Classify the candidate as product workflow, operating cadence, research
   method, advisory persona, or executive communication.
3. Prefer coverage by existing trusted business, commerce, research, or design
   skills when the candidate is broad or persona-like.
4. Convert only concrete workflows with measurable artifacts such as PRDs,
   experiment plans, teardown matrices, roadmaps, scorecards, or decision memos.
5. Mark unsupported financial, legal, HR, or executive claims as assumptions
   requiring owner review.

## Expected Output

- candidate cluster and intended business artifact
- evidence, assumptions, and decision-owner map
- existing trusted skill or bundle coverage
- recommended workflow stages and gate criteria
- adoption decision and residual risks

## Verifier Expectations

- unsupported claim check
- duplicate trusted-skill check
- decision-owner and artifact check
- candidate-map coverage check

## Failure Handling

If the candidate is only a broad advisor persona, keep it covered by this
cluster skill unless a concrete recurring artifact justifies a separate local
skill.

# Compliance Accessibility Policy

## When To Use

Use this skill when preparing or reviewing accessibility policy language,
support commitments, or conformance notes.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify product scope, audience, accessibility target, and review owner.
2. Check that commitments match actual product support and verification evidence.
3. Separate policy wording from UI remediation tasks.
4. Record known limitations and support contact or escalation process.
5. Avoid claiming full compliance without evidence.

## Expected Output

- policy review notes
- commitment and evidence mapping
- limitation notes
- remediation or owner questions

## Verifier Expectations

- evidence check
- product scope check
- support path check
- overclaim review

## Failure Handling

If conformance evidence is unavailable, provide a draft marked as unverified.

# Content Editorial Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when an article, page, post, script, or internal draft needs an
editorial pass before publication or handoff.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify audience, channel, goal, length, and required tone.
2. Check structure, clarity, repetition, unsupported claims, and source needs.
3. Preserve the author's factual intent while improving readability.
4. Separate content risks from style preferences.
5. Provide concise edits or a revision plan.

## Expected Output

- editorial findings
- revised copy or change plan
- factual claim notes
- publication readiness status

## Verifier Expectations

- audience fit check
- factual consistency check
- tone and structure check
- source need check

## Failure Handling

If the target audience is unclear, state the assumed audience before editing.

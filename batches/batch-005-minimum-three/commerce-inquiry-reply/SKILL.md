# Commerce Inquiry Reply

## When To Use

Use this skill when a buyer or lead asks about product details, price, shipping,
MOQ, samples, customization, or documents.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify buyer question, product facts, missing details, and business policy.
2. Answer confirmed facts directly and mark unknowns as follow-up questions.
3. Keep tone professional, specific, and action-oriented.
4. Avoid unsupported delivery, certification, warranty, or performance claims.
5. Provide next-step options such as sample request, specification confirmation,
   or quotation preparation.

## Expected Output

- reply draft
- missing information list
- risk or claim warnings
- next-step checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- product fact check
- policy and claim check
- buyer question coverage
- clear next step

## Failure Handling

If key business terms are unavailable, prepare a safe draft that asks for the
missing details.

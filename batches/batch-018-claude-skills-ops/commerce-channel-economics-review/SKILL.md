# Commerce Channel Economics Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when commerce, finance, RevOps, or partnership teams need to
review reseller, referral, marketplace, or channel unit economics.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify channel type, customer segment, price, margin, incentives, cost
   allocation, and reporting period.
2. Separate gross margin, partner payout, acquisition cost, support cost,
   implementation load, and payback assumptions.
3. Check formulas, denominator consistency, one-time effects, and attribution
   before comparing channels.
4. Flag accounting, tax, legal, and incentive-risk questions for specialist
   review.
5. Produce scenario findings with caveats and measurement plan.

## Expected Output

- channel economics summary
- formula and assumption map
- margin and payback findings
- risk and sensitivity notes
- measurement checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- formula consistency check
- margin and cost allocation check
- attribution check
- regulated-advice boundary check

## Failure Handling

If cost or attribution data is incomplete, provide a scenario model outline and
avoid precise ROI claims.

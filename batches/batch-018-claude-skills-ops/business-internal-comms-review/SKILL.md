# Business Internal Comms Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when operations, leadership, HR, product, or incident teams need
to review internal communications, change announcements, or alignment messages.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify audience, message owner, timing, channel, decision context, and
   required approvals.
2. Separate facts, decisions, rationale, action required, timing, risks, and
   open questions.
3. Check clarity, audience fit, confidentiality, legal or HR sensitivity, and
   unsupported claims.
4. Preserve source-of-truth links and escalation contacts.
5. Produce a message review with revisions, caveats, and approval needs.

## Expected Output

- audience and objective summary
- clarity and completeness findings
- risk and sensitivity notes
- revision recommendations
- approval checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- audience-action check
- source-of-truth check
- sensitivity and confidentiality check
- approval boundary check

## Failure Handling

If facts or approvals are missing, mark the communication as draft and list
confirmation needs.

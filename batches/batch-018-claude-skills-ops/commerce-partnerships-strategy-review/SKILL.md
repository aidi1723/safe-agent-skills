# Commerce Partnerships Strategy Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a business development, partnerships, sales, or product team
needs to review partner strategy, alliance fit, channel motions, or ecosystem
tradeoffs.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify partner type, target segment, value exchange, ownership, incentives,
   and success metrics.
2. Separate strategic fit, customer value, revenue potential, operational load,
   channel conflict, and legal or privacy risks.
3. Check assumptions against evidence such as pipeline, customer overlap,
   delivery capacity, and partner commitments.
4. Flag exclusivity, data sharing, brand, compliance, and authority questions.
5. Produce an options matrix with test plan, risks, and decision criteria.

## Expected Output

- partnership fit summary
- value exchange and incentive review
- channel conflict and risk notes
- pilot or rollout criteria
- approval checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- evidence support check
- channel conflict check
- data and brand boundary check
- commitment authority check

## Failure Handling

If partner evidence is sparse, frame recommendations as hypotheses and define a
pilot before broad rollout.

# Commerce Pricing Strategy Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when product, sales, finance, or commerce teams need to review
pricing structure, packaging, discount logic, or monetization tradeoffs.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify customer segment, offer structure, cost drivers, value drivers, and
   competitive context.
2. Separate list price, discount policy, contract terms, margin, and willingness
   to pay assumptions.
3. Check whether proposed prices align with evidence from usage, customer
   interviews, win-loss data, or transaction history.
4. Flag legal, fairness, channel-conflict, and margin-risk issues for specialist
   review when relevant.
5. Recommend testable pricing options with measurement criteria and rollback
   triggers.

## Expected Output

- pricing context summary
- package and discounting review
- margin and evidence caveats
- test options and metrics
- escalation risks

## Verifier Expectations

- unit economics check
- evidence support check
- discount and margin consistency check
- legal and channel-risk escalation check

## Failure Handling

If pricing data is sparse, frame recommendations as hypotheses and define the
evidence needed before rollout.

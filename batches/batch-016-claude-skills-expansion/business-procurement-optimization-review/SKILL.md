# Business Procurement Optimization Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a procurement, operations, or finance team needs to assess
supplier spend, sourcing choices, vendor risk, or purchase-process quality.

## Safe Workflow

1. Define the category, business need, spend baseline, constraints, and decision
   timeline.
2. Segment spend by supplier, contract, department, geography, and renewal date
   when data is available.
3. Evaluate savings, service quality, continuity risk, switching cost, and
   compliance requirements separately.
4. Identify negotiation levers and process fixes without directing deceptive or
   coercive supplier practices.
5. Produce an options matrix with evidence, tradeoffs, and approval boundaries.

## Expected Output

- procurement baseline summary
- supplier and category risk matrix
- savings and process opportunity list
- sourcing decision tradeoffs
- approval and evidence checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- spend baseline check
- supplier risk check
- contract and approval boundary check
- unsupported savings claim check

## Failure Handling

If spend or contract data is missing, provide a diagnostic checklist and avoid
quantifying savings beyond the evidence.

# Business Customer Success Health Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when customer success, support, sales, or product teams need to
review customer health, churn risk, renewal readiness, or expansion signals.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify account segment, lifecycle stage, contract timing, product usage,
   support history, and stakeholder signals.
2. Separate observed evidence from sentiment, inferred risk, and planned action.
3. Review adoption, business outcome progress, support burden, relationship
   coverage, renewal blockers, and expansion fit.
4. Flag privacy, contractual, and unsupported scoring assumptions.
5. Produce a health narrative with prioritized interventions and verification
   checks.

## Expected Output

- customer health summary
- churn and renewal risk drivers
- adoption and outcome gaps
- prioritized success actions
- data caveats and follow-up checks

## Verifier Expectations

- evidence-to-score traceability check
- lifecycle and renewal date check
- privacy boundary check
- unsupported risk inference check

## Failure Handling

If account data is incomplete, provide a confidence-rated health review and list
the missing signals needed before escalation.

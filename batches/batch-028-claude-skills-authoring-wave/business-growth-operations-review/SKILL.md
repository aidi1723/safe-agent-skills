# Business Growth Operations Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when reviewing growth work across acquisition, activation,
conversion, retention, expansion, channel mix, experiment cadence, and
operating ownership.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the growth goal, target segment, funnel stage, channels, baseline
   metrics, source systems, owners, and decision horizon.
2. Separate acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, expansion, referral,
   and lifecycle efforts before comparing performance.
3. Check event definitions, attribution windows, cohort mix, sample size,
   experiment design, seasonality, and channel spend before drawing conclusions.
4. Distinguish measured evidence from hypotheses, benchmarks, and assumptions.
5. Convert findings into prioritized tests, instrumentation fixes, owner
   actions, and stop or scale decisions.

## Expected Output

- growth funnel or loop map
- metric and instrumentation findings
- experiment and channel notes
- prioritized opportunities
- decision and owner checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- metric definition check
- attribution and cohort check
- experiment validity check
- claims and evidence boundary check

## Failure Handling

If measurement is weak or attribution is unclear, provide a learning plan and
avoid claiming causal lift.

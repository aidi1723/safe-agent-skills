# Research Operations Governance Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when research operations need a governance review across study
intake, participant handling, consent boundaries, evidence management,
repository hygiene, insight delivery, and stakeholder handoffs.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the research type, participants or sources, jurisdictional context,
   study owner, tools, consent needs, data sensitivity, and intended decision.
2. Map intake, prioritization, recruitment or source selection, consent,
   collection, analysis, storage, synthesis, delivery, and retention steps.
3. Check whether consent, privacy, de-identification, evidence lineage,
   repository permissions, and review gates match the sensitivity of the work.
4. Separate operational governance findings from legal, medical, ethics-board,
   or clinical advice; escalate regulated research questions.
5. Convert findings into governance controls, repository fixes, evidence
   standards, owner actions, and decision-ready insight requirements.

## Expected Output

- research operations workflow map
- participant and evidence governance findings
- repository and permission gaps
- consent and escalation notes
- owner action checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- consent and sensitivity check
- evidence lineage check
- repository permission check
- regulated-research boundary check

## Failure Handling

If consent, source lineage, or retention records are missing, limit conclusions
to process risk and list the records needed before further use.

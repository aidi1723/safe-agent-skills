# Commerce Commercial Operations Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a commercial team needs to review operating workflows that
connect pipeline, buyer qualification, pricing, approvals, delivery readiness,
and revenue handoffs.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the commercial motion, buyer segment, revenue model, owners, tools,
   approval gates, and decision that the review should support.
2. Map the workflow from lead or opportunity intake through qualification,
   pricing, contract review, fulfillment handoff, and renewal or expansion.
3. Check whether stage definitions, exit criteria, discount authority, risk
   flags, and handoff responsibilities are explicit and consistently used.
4. Separate commercial process findings from legal, accounting, tax, or
   investment advice; escalate regulated questions to qualified owners.
5. Convert gaps into operating decisions, owner actions, controls, and metrics
   instead of generic sales advice.

## Expected Output

- commercial workflow map
- qualification and approval findings
- handoff and ownership gaps
- revenue risk and control notes
- prioritized operating actions

## Verifier Expectations

- stage definition check
- approval authority check
- handoff owner check
- regulated-advice boundary check

## Failure Handling

If the team lacks pipeline, pricing, or approval evidence, produce a caveated
operating review and list the minimum records needed for a defensible decision.

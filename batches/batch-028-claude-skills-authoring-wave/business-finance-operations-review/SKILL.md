# Business Finance Operations Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a team needs to review how finance work is operated:
budget ownership, forecast cadence, spend controls, variance review, cash
planning, reporting handoffs, and decision governance.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the business model, reporting period, finance owners, source
   systems, approval policies, and decisions the finance process supports.
2. Separate planning, procurement, spend approval, close, forecast update,
   variance review, cash review, and management reporting steps.
3. Check formulas, source reconciliation, approval thresholds, timing, version
   control, owner accountability, and one-time items before interpreting
   variance or runway signals.
4. Present operational finance findings, not investment, audit, accounting,
   tax, or legal advice.
5. Translate findings into cadence changes, control gaps, data requirements,
   escalation points, and owner actions.

## Expected Output

- finance operating workflow map
- forecast and budget control findings
- variance and cash governance notes
- data and reconciliation gaps
- owner action checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- source reconciliation check
- formula and period check
- approval threshold check
- regulated-advice boundary check

## Failure Handling

If source finance data or approval records are incomplete, avoid numeric
conclusions beyond the evidence and provide a structured data request.

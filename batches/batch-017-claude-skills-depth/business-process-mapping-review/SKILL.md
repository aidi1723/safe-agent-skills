# Business Process Mapping Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a team needs to map or review business processes, handoffs,
bottlenecks, SOP gaps, operating workflows, or cross-functional improvement
plans.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify process scope, trigger, actors, systems, inputs, outputs, handoffs,
   and decision points.
2. Separate current state, target state, pain points, controls, exceptions, and
   automation ideas.
3. Check for missing owners, circular handoffs, unverified assumptions, control
   gaps, and unclear success metrics.
4. Recommend process changes with evidence, risk, and rollout checks.
5. Keep compliance, HR, legal, and production changes behind appropriate review.

## Expected Output

- current-state process map summary
- bottleneck and handoff findings
- control and ownership gaps
- improvement options
- rollout verification checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- actor and handoff check
- input-output consistency check
- control gap check
- approval boundary check

## Failure Handling

If stakeholders or source process evidence are missing, provide a mapping
questionnaire and avoid asserting the process as complete.

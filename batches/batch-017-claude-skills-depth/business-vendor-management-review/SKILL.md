# Business Vendor Management Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when an operations, procurement, IT, or finance team needs to
review vendor health, supplier performance, renewal readiness, or third-party
risk.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify vendor scope, contract owner, service category, renewal date,
   dependency level, and available evidence.
2. Separate service performance, commercial value, security risk, compliance
   obligations, and relationship health.
3. Compare SLA claims against source records before recommending credits,
   escalation, or renewal changes.
4. Flag single-source dependency, data access, business continuity, and contract
   issues for specialist review.
5. Produce a scorecard with caveats, owner actions, and approval boundaries.

## Expected Output

- vendor scorecard
- renewal and dependency risk notes
- SLA and evidence gaps
- owner action list
- escalation boundaries

## Verifier Expectations

- contract and SLA evidence check
- third-party risk check
- renewal date check
- unsupported claim check

## Failure Handling

If vendor evidence is incomplete, produce a confidence-rated review and list the
minimum records needed before renewal or escalation decisions.

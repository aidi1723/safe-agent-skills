# Commerce Deal Desk Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when sales, finance, legal, or operations teams need to review a
deal approval package, discount exception, quote, or non-standard commercial
term.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify customer, product scope, pricing, discount, term length, approvals,
   exceptions, and deadline.
2. Separate margin impact, revenue timing, legal terms, implementation risk,
   support commitments, and strategic value.
3. Check whether claims, discounts, and concessions match policy and evidence.
4. Flag legal, privacy, security, accounting, and authority issues for
   specialist approval.
5. Produce an approval-ready summary with risks, alternatives, and missing
   evidence.

## Expected Output

- deal summary
- discount and margin review
- exception and approval checklist
- risk and evidence gaps
- recommended next actions

## Verifier Expectations

- approval policy check
- pricing and margin check
- unsupported commitment check
- legal and accounting escalation check

## Failure Handling

If deal data is incomplete, mark the package not approval-ready and list the
missing evidence.

# Business Financial Analysis Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a team needs to review business financial analysis,
operating metrics, budget variance, unit economics, profitability, or cash
planning narratives.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify period, source statements, accounting basis, business model, and
   decision context.
2. Separate revenue, cost, margin, cash, working capital, budget variance, and
   unit economics.
3. Check formulas, time windows, one-time effects, allocations, and source
   reconciliation before drawing conclusions.
4. Present caveated business analysis, not investment, tax, audit, or accounting
   advice.
5. Record assumptions, missing data, and specialist escalation needs.

## Expected Output

- financial analysis summary
- metric and formula checks
- variance and driver notes
- caveats and missing data
- decision support questions

## Verifier Expectations

- source reconciliation check
- formula and period check
- one-time item check
- regulated-advice boundary check

## Failure Handling

If source financial data is incomplete, provide a structured analysis template
and avoid numeric conclusions beyond the evidence.

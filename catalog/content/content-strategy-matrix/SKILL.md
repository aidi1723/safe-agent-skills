# Content Strategy Matrix

## When To Use

Use this skill when content work needs a repeatable strategy rather than a
single draft: topic pillars, buyer or user stages, channel formats, SEO or GEO
intent, short-form scripts, social posts, email, product copy, and repurposing
paths.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify audience segments, goals, product facts, channels, constraints, and
   approval boundaries.
2. Define content pillars and the role of each pillar in awareness, education,
   conversion, support, or retention.
3. Build a matrix across audience stage, channel, format, message angle,
   evidence required, CTA, owner, and freshness window.
4. Separate factual claims from positioning, opinion, and creative hooks.
5. Mark source requirements, compliance-sensitive claims, brand voice risks,
   and reuse opportunities.
6. Convert the matrix into a prioritized production queue with review gates.

## Expected Output

- content pillars and audience-stage map
- channel and format matrix
- claim and evidence requirements
- production priority queue
- review, freshness, and compliance notes

## Verifier Expectations

- audience and goal check
- source-backed claim check
- brand voice check
- freshness and channel-fit review
- compliance-sensitive language check when public claims are present

## Failure Handling

If audience or product facts are missing, produce a strategy scaffold with
explicit unknowns instead of inventing claims. If the content is public or
regulated, route risky claims through compliance review.

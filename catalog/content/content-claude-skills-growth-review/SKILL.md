# Content Claude Skills Growth Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a backlog candidate concerns copywriting, marketing ideas,
demand acquisition, page CRO, paywall CRO, churn prevention, app-store
optimization, campaign analytics, social growth, or content strategy.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify audience, channel, funnel stage, claim sensitivity, target action,
   and measurable success metric.
2. Separate content generation, positioning, analytics review, experiment
   design, and compliance-sensitive claims.
3. Prefer existing content, SEO, social, claims-compliance, or brand-voice
   skills unless the candidate provides a distinct artifact.
4. Convert candidates into bounded outputs such as briefs, test matrices,
   landing-page audits, lifecycle messages, ASO checklists, or campaign notes.
5. Require fact, claim, privacy, and platform-policy review before publication.

## Expected Output

- channel and funnel context
- content artifact contract
- claim and compliance risk notes
- experiment or metric plan
- adoption decision and trusted-skill coverage

## Verifier Expectations

- unsupported marketing claim check
- platform or publication boundary check
- duplicate content-skill check
- candidate-map coverage check

## Failure Handling

If success metrics, audience, or claim evidence are missing, produce a content
brief and validation plan before drafting final public copy.

# SEO Content Evidence

Use this guide to turn search demand into a differentiated, source-bounded
content brief.

## Intent And Page Role

Record audience, market, language, page type, primary intent, secondary
questions, funnel role, and conversion action. Define what the page will answer
that existing or competing pages do not.

## Queries And Entities

Assign primary and supporting queries, entities, terminology, comparisons, and
questions to specific sections. Avoid keyword repetition that harms clarity or
creates overlapping pages. Record canonical intent and internal link targets.

## Claims And Freshness

Map factual, legal, technical, medical, financial, product, and time-sensitive
claims to approved sources and freshness requirements. Separate positioning
from evidence and list prohibited or unverified claims.

## Handoff

Provide title direction, heading structure, section purpose, evidence needs,
voice, accessibility notes, links, CTA boundary, and technical SEO dependencies
that still need separate implementation and verification.

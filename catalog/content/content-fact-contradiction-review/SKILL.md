# Content Fact Contradiction Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when content includes multiple facts, dates, prices, versions,
commitments, entities, or causal claims that must remain consistent.

## Safe Workflow

1. Extract concrete claims: names, dates, numbers, prices, feature statements,
   obligations, limitations, and cause-effect assertions.
2. Compare claims across sections for conflicts, drift, missing definitions,
   and incompatible assumptions.
3. Mark claims as sourced, inferred, unsupported, or contradictory.
4. Resolve contradictions by preserving the source-backed claim and flagging
   uncertainty instead of inventing a reconciliation.
5. Record open questions that require source or owner confirmation.

## Expected Output

- extracted claim list
- contradiction findings
- unsupported claim notes
- recommended corrections
- unresolved source questions

## Verifier Expectations

- claim inventory check
- internal consistency check
- source support check
- uncertainty disclosure check

## Failure Handling

If source material is unavailable, limit the review to internal consistency and
label factual accuracy as unverified.

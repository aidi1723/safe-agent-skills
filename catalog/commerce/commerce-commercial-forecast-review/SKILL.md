# Commerce Commercial Forecast Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a sales, RevOps, finance, or leadership team needs to review
commercial forecasts, pipeline assumptions, or board-ready revenue narratives.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify forecast period, segments, pipeline stages, historical conversion
   windows, renewal assumptions, and ownership.
2. Separate committed business, best case, pipeline-only, renewals, expansion,
   and one-time effects.
3. Check conversion, slippage, seasonality, cohort, and stage-aging assumptions
   before accepting a forecast number.
4. Surface uncertainty ranges and scenario drivers instead of presenting a
   single unsupported point estimate.
5. Keep financial reporting, audit, and investor guidance decisions outside the
   skill boundary.

## Expected Output

- forecast assumption map
- scenario and confidence notes
- pipeline quality findings
- data gaps and caveats
- board narrative risks

## Verifier Expectations

- historical conversion check
- period and stage consistency check
- unsupported forecast claim check
- finance boundary check

## Failure Handling

If forecast inputs are incomplete, produce a scenario checklist and mark the
forecast as low confidence.

# Commerce Commercial Policy Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when sales, finance, legal, or operations teams need to review
commercial policy, discount rules, approval thresholds, or exception handling.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify policy scope, owner, effective date, affected teams, and approval
   authority.
2. Separate pricing, discounting, contract terms, revenue recognition, support
   commitments, and exception paths.
3. Check whether policy language is testable, enforceable, and traceable to
   business risk.
4. Flag legal, accounting, compliance, and authority questions for specialist
   review.
5. Produce policy findings with examples, gaps, and governance checkpoints.

## Expected Output

- policy scope summary
- ambiguity and exception findings
- approval and evidence gaps
- risk and escalation notes
- update checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- authority and owner check
- exception path check
- unsupported commitment check
- legal and accounting boundary check

## Failure Handling

If policy ownership or source terms are unclear, mark the review as draft and
list the confirming stakeholders.

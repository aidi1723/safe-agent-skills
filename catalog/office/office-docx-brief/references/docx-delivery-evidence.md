# DOCX Delivery Evidence

Use this guide for a document that must remain editable and render reliably.

## Content Contract

Record audience, purpose, required sections, approved sources, assumptions,
terms that must remain exact, and any comments or tracked changes that constrain
editing. Separate sourced facts from generated prose.

## Structure And Styles

Use semantic heading, body, list, table, caption, quote, and reference styles.
Review section breaks, headers/footers, page numbering, tables, images, fields,
links, footnotes, and embedded objects that affect the document contract.

## Render Review

Render the DOCX and inspect representative pages plus dense tables, lists,
figures, and section transitions. Check pagination, orphan headings, clipping,
overflow, spacing, font substitution, and repeated header/footer content.

## Delivery Check

Reopen the file, verify required sections and links, preserve the original,
record output identity, and state any unresolved tracked content, layout drift,
unsupported facts, or export risk.

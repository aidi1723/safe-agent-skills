# Business Support Triage

## When To Use

Use this skill when a task involves customer support tickets, reply drafts,
issue classification, escalation routing, or support workflow summaries.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the customer issue, product area, urgency, and requested outcome.
2. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
3. Avoid exposing private customer data outside approved systems.
4. Draft replies that are clear, polite, and bounded by known policy.
5. Escalate billing, legal, security, safety, account access, or high-risk complaints.
6. Record missing information needed for a final response.

## Expected Output

- issue category
- urgency and escalation recommendation
- draft reply or internal note
- missing-input checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- policy checklist
- tone and format check
- escalation requirement check

## Failure Handling

If policy or customer facts are missing, mark the reply as draft-only and
request the missing inputs.

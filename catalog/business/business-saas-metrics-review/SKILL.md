# Business SaaS Metrics Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a SaaS business needs a metrics review, dashboard critique,
or operating narrative around recurring revenue and customer retention.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the reporting period, customer segments, pricing model, and data
   sources.
2. Separate revenue metrics, retention metrics, acquisition metrics, and
   efficiency metrics.
3. Check formula definitions before comparing numbers across teams or periods.
4. Flag missing denominators, cohort mix shifts, one-time revenue, and
   non-comparable time windows.
5. Convert findings into decisions, risks, and follow-up analyses rather than
   investment advice.

## Expected Output

- metric definition table
- dashboard quality findings
- growth, retention, and efficiency notes
- data gaps and caveats
- decision-oriented next checks

## Verifier Expectations

- formula definition check
- period and cohort consistency check
- source reconciliation check
- financial-advice boundary check

## Failure Handling

If source data is incomplete, produce a caveated metrics review and list the
minimum data needed for a defensible conclusion.

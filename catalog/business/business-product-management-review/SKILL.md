# Business Product Management Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when product, design, engineering, or leadership teams need to
review product strategy, roadmap tradeoffs, discovery evidence, or feature
prioritization.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify customer segment, problem, evidence, constraints, roadmap horizon,
   and decision owner.
2. Separate user evidence, business impact, technical risk, delivery effort,
   dependencies, and open assumptions.
3. Check whether PMF, retention, revenue, or adoption claims are backed by
   concrete signals.
4. Compare options with explicit tradeoffs and stop conditions.
5. Produce recommendations as decision support, not unilateral product
   direction.

## Expected Output

- product decision context
- evidence and assumption map
- prioritization tradeoffs
- risk and dependency notes
- validation or de-scope plan

## Verifier Expectations

- customer evidence check
- success metric check
- dependency check
- unsupported PMF claim check

## Failure Handling

If discovery evidence is weak, produce a learning plan before roadmap
commitment.

# Business Revenue Operations Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a RevOps, sales, marketing, finance, or leadership team
needs to review pipeline operations, funnel health, or go-to-market efficiency.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify funnel stages, CRM definitions, source systems, ownership, segments,
   and time window.
2. Separate pipeline quality, forecast accuracy, conversion efficiency,
   activity coverage, and data hygiene.
3. Check stage definitions, duplicate records, stale opportunities, missing
   close dates, and inconsistent attribution.
4. Translate findings into process fixes, dashboard changes, and follow-up
   analyses rather than unsupported revenue promises.
5. Record data lineage and assumptions for every metric.

## Expected Output

- RevOps health summary
- funnel and pipeline findings
- CRM hygiene issues
- metric definition gaps
- prioritized operating fixes

## Verifier Expectations

- CRM stage definition check
- data freshness check
- metric formula check
- unsupported revenue claim check

## Failure Handling

If CRM data is unavailable or inconsistent, provide a cleanup plan before
forecast or efficiency conclusions.

# Business Knowledge Operations Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a team needs to review internal knowledge operations,
documentation quality, SOP discoverability, support knowledge bases, or
organizational knowledge gaps.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify audiences, knowledge domains, source owners, freshness expectations,
   and retrieval or search paths.
2. Separate authoritative policy, operating SOP, tribal knowledge, customer
   support content, and draft guidance.
3. Check ownership, duplication, stale content, missing source links, and access
   boundaries.
4. Recommend information architecture and maintenance routines before tooling
   changes.
5. Produce a prioritized knowledge ops backlog with verification criteria.

## Expected Output

- knowledge inventory summary
- gap and duplication findings
- ownership and freshness risks
- IA or taxonomy recommendations
- maintenance backlog

## Verifier Expectations

- owner and source check
- freshness check
- access boundary check
- duplicate guidance check

## Failure Handling

If the knowledge base cannot be inventoried, provide a sampling plan and mark
coverage as incomplete.

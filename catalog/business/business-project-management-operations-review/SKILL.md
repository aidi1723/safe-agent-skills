# Business Project Management Operations Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a project, program, or cross-functional initiative needs a
review of delivery cadence, scope control, dependencies, risks, decisions,
stakeholder alignment, and execution governance.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the objective, scope, owners, milestones, artifacts, stakeholders,
   decision rights, delivery method, and current status.
2. Map workstreams, dependencies, decision gates, risk owners, communication
   cadence, change control, and acceptance criteria.
3. Check whether status, dates, blockers, scope changes, assumptions, and
   dependencies are evidence-backed and current.
4. Separate delivery process recommendations from HR, legal, procurement, or
   contractual advice; escalate specialist issues.
5. Turn gaps into decision requests, risk treatments, owner actions, timeline
   changes, or scope tradeoffs.

## Expected Output

- project operating map
- status and dependency findings
- risk and decision log review
- scope and cadence recommendations
- owner action plan

## Verifier Expectations

- objective and scope check
- dependency owner check
- status evidence check
- escalation boundary check

## Failure Handling

If the project record is stale or fragmented, produce a recovery checklist and
identify the minimum artifacts needed for a reliable plan.

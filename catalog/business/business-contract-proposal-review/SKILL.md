# Business Contract Proposal Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when sales, legal, delivery, or operations teams need to review
proposal or contract language before stakeholder approval.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify customer, scope, commercial model, delivery owner, approval path,
   and source documents.
2. Separate offer language, assumptions, exclusions, acceptance criteria,
   timelines, pricing, support, and renewal terms.
3. Check for unsupported commitments, vague deliverables, conflicting terms,
   missing dependencies, and unapproved exceptions.
4. Flag legal, procurement, finance, security, or delivery risks for specialist
   review.
5. Produce a decision-support review, not legal advice or final contract terms.

## Expected Output

- scope and offer summary
- ambiguity and commitment findings
- assumption and dependency register
- specialist review flags
- approval checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- source document check
- unsupported commitment check
- acceptance criteria check
- legal and finance boundary check

## Failure Handling

If source terms conflict, stop short of rewriting final language and list the
terms requiring owner confirmation.

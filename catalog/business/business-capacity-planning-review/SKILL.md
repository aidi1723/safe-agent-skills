# Business Capacity Planning Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when teams need to review capacity, workload, staffing
assumptions, delivery commitments, resourcing tradeoffs, or operational
bottlenecks.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify planning horizon, teams, commitments, demand sources, constraints,
   and available capacity data.
2. Separate recurring work, project work, interrupts, support load, hiring
   assumptions, and dependencies.
3. Check utilization, queue age, service levels, skill constraints, and hidden
   work before accepting capacity claims.
4. Present scenarios and tradeoffs without making HR, budget, or employment
   decisions.
5. Produce an action plan with assumptions, risks, and verification metrics.

## Expected Output

- capacity baseline summary
- demand and constraint findings
- scenario tradeoffs
- bottleneck and risk notes
- verification checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- demand-source check
- utilization and hidden-work check
- dependency check
- HR and budget boundary check

## Failure Handling

If capacity data is missing, produce a data collection plan and mark forecasts
as low confidence.

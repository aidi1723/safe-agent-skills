# Business Value Investment Research Framework

## When To Use

Use this skill when preparing or reviewing investment research, value-investing
diligence, business quality analysis, capital allocation memos, or portfolio
decision support.

This skill provides a research framework only. It is not financial, legal, tax,
or investment advice.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify the decision context, asset type, owner, time horizon, capital at
   risk, jurisdiction, and required specialist review.
2. Split the memo into business quality, management, moat, unit economics,
   valuation, downside case, catalysts, and exit conditions.
3. Require each major claim to cite a filing, transcript, audited statement,
   primary data source, or clearly labeled assumption.
4. Run at least two lenses: base thesis and adversarial bear case. Keep them
   separate until the decision-readiness summary.
5. State valuation assumptions explicitly: revenue, margin, reinvestment,
   discount rate or multiple, terminal value, dilution, and downside range.
6. Flag missing data, conflicts of interest, liquidity constraints,
   concentration risk, and model sensitivity.
7. End with decision-readiness, not a buy/sell/hold instruction.

## Expected Output

- investment research scope
- evidence and assumption map
- thesis and bear-case summary
- valuation sensitivity notes
- diligence gap register
- non-advice and specialist-review boundary

## Verifier Expectations

- primary-source and assumption check
- downside and sensitivity check
- conflict and concentration risk check
- regulated-advice boundary check
- decision-owner and approval check

## Failure Handling

If evidence, valuation assumptions, or decision authority are incomplete, mark
the research as not decision-ready and list the missing diligence.

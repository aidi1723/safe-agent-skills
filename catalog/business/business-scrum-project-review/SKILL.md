# Business Scrum Project Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a product, engineering, or operations team needs to review
Scrum execution, sprint health, backlog quality, delivery risk, or project
operating rhythm.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify team scope, sprint dates, goals, backlog, blockers, dependencies,
   and delivery commitments.
2. Separate discovery work, committed sprint work, operational interrupts,
   defects, and external dependencies.
3. Check story readiness, acceptance criteria, WIP, carryover, blocker age, and
   decision ownership.
4. Recommend operating improvements without pretending process metrics alone
   prove productivity.
5. Produce a delivery risk review with concrete follow-up actions.

## Expected Output

- sprint or project health summary
- backlog and blocker findings
- dependency and delivery risks
- ceremony or workflow improvements
- next-action owner list

## Verifier Expectations

- sprint goal alignment check
- acceptance criteria check
- dependency check
- metric misuse check

## Failure Handling

If project data is sparse, provide a focused diagnostic checklist and mark
delivery confidence as unknown.

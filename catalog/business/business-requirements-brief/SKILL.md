# Business Requirements Brief

## When To Use

Use this skill when a product, operations, or internal project needs clear
requirements before execution.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify stakeholders, objective, users, constraints, and success criteria.
2. Separate must-have requirements from preferences and open questions.
3. Capture assumptions and dependencies.
4. Convert vague requests into testable acceptance criteria.
5. Keep scope boundaries explicit.

## Expected Output

- requirements summary
- acceptance criteria
- assumptions
- open questions
- scope boundaries

## Verifier Expectations

- stakeholder objective check
- acceptance criteria check
- dependency check
- ambiguity review

## Failure Handling

If the business goal is unclear, draft a minimal requirements brief with marked
assumptions.

# Compliance Claude Skills Regulated Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a backlog candidate concerns regulated-domain review,
quality management, privacy, AI governance, medical-device processes, security
management, audit preparation, or corrective action workflows.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify jurisdiction, standard, artifact, decision owner, evidence source,
   and review purpose.
2. State that the output is review support and evidence organization, not legal,
   medical, certification, or regulatory authority.
3. Convert broad specialist roles into bounded checklists, evidence matrices,
   gap logs, and owner-review questions.
4. Require primary-source references or operator-provided policy text before
   making standard-specific claims.
5. Escalate unclear or high-stakes conclusions to qualified human review.

## Expected Output

- regulated-domain scope and jurisdiction notes
- evidence and artifact checklist
- gap, assumption, and owner-review log
- applicable trusted-skill coverage
- adoption decision and escalation notes

## Verifier Expectations

- authority-boundary check
- primary-source or provided-policy check
- evidence matrix check
- candidate-map coverage check

## Failure Handling

If the standard, jurisdiction, or qualified reviewer is unclear, produce only a
scope-and-evidence checklist and mark conclusions unresolved.

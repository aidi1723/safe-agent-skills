# Compliance Terms Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when user-facing policy, terms, disclaimers, or operational
commitments need risk review.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify document scope, audience, jurisdiction assumption, and business use.
2. Check obligations, prohibited claims, refund or warranty language, privacy
   references, and conflict with product behavior.
3. Separate legal risk flags from plain-language clarity issues.
4. Avoid giving legal advice; provide review notes for counsel or owner.
5. Record unknown assumptions and approval needs.

## Expected Output

- risk findings
- unclear or conflicting clauses
- owner review questions
- approval checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- scope and audience check
- obligation and claim check
- consistency with product behavior
- counsel or owner review note

## Failure Handling

If jurisdiction or policy owner is unknown, mark the review as preliminary.

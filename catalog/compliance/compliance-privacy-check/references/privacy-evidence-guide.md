# Privacy Evidence Guide

Use this guide to structure an operational privacy review. It does not replace
qualified legal interpretation or the organization's approved policies.

## Data Map

Record the data subject, fields, sensitivity, source, purpose, processing
operation, recipient, storage location, retention period, deletion path, and
accountable owner. Include free text, logs, identifiers, derived attributes,
and metadata that may enable linkage.

## Control Evidence

Collect the applicable policy or approved procedure, access-control evidence,
redaction or minimization method, retention configuration, transfer boundary,
audit trail, and approval record. State whether the evidence describes the
current system or only an intended design.

## Decision Questions

- Can the purpose be met with fewer fields, shorter retention, aggregation, or
  local processing?
- Can a recipient identify a person directly or by linking other available
  information?
- Are minors, employees, health, financial, biometric, location, or other
  sensitive categories involved?
- Does the workflow cross an organization, account, region, jurisdiction, or
  approved processing boundary?
- Who can approve the use, and who owns deletion or incident response?

## Escalation Triggers

Stop before disclosure or irreversible processing when the policy scope,
authority, recipient, jurisdiction, retention basis, or data classification is
unknown. Record the exact missing evidence and route it to the accountable
privacy or compliance owner. Do not convert uncertainty into a legal
conclusion.

# Compliance Public Claim Risk Register

## When To Use

Use this skill when public-facing content includes claims about performance,
security, legality, pricing, availability, certifications, guarantees, or
customer outcomes.

## Safe Workflow

1. Inventory each public claim, target audience, channel, owner, supporting
   evidence, and required review path.
2. Classify claims by risk: factual, comparative, forward-looking, regulatory,
   customer-result, security-sensitive, or commercial promise.
3. Check for absolute wording, unsupported superiority claims, missing scope,
   missing conditions, and outdated evidence.
4. Separate editorial edits from claims that require legal, compliance,
   security, or product-owner review.
5. Record required disclaimers, evidence gaps, owner decisions, and residual
   publication risk.

## Expected Output

- public claim register
- risk classification
- evidence and owner map
- review-required claim list
- disclaimer or wording recommendations

## Verifier Expectations

- claim inventory check
- evidence support check
- risk classification check
- owner or review path check

## Failure Handling

If ownership or evidence is missing for a high-risk claim, keep it out of the
publishable draft until review is complete.

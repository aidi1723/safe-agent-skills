# Vibe Trading Research Assistant

## When To Use

Use this skill when reviewing an AI-assisted trading or quantitative research
workflow that summarizes market data, explains signals, or prepares analysis
notes for human review.

## Safe Workflow

1. Define the work as research support, not financial advice.
2. Separate factual market data, model assumptions, strategy logic, and opinion.
3. Require source, date, market, symbol, and data freshness for every claim.
4. Flag leverage, derivatives, illiquid assets, backtest overfitting, and missing
   risk controls.
5. Do not place trades, produce private investment recommendations, or promise
   returns.
6. End with uncertainty, limitations, and human-review requirements.

## Expected Output

- research summary
- data provenance
- strategy assumptions
- risk notes
- compliance disclaimer
- human decision checkpoint

## Verifier Expectations

- no trade execution
- no guaranteed return claims
- no personalized financial advice
- data sources and dates are explicit

## Boundary

This is a high-risk reference skill inspired by Vibe-Trading-style community
projects. It is for research review only and must stay review-required unless a
separate compliance approval process exists.

# Research Paper Synthesis

## When To Use

Use this skill when a task needs a structured read of academic papers, technical
reports, or benchmark writeups.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify title, authors, venue, date, and link for each source.
2. Extract claim, method, dataset, evaluation, limitations, and reproducibility
   notes.
3. Separate what the paper proves from what it suggests.
4. Compare methods only on matching assumptions.
5. Cite sources for every important technical claim.

## Expected Output

- paper summary
- method and evidence table
- limitation notes
- practical takeaway

## Verifier Expectations

- source citation check
- date and author check
- method versus result separation
- limitation coverage

## Failure Handling

If the paper cannot be accessed, summarize only available metadata and state the
access gap.

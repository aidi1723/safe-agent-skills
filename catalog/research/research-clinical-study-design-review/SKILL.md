# Research Clinical Study Design Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when reviewing clinical research plans, protocols, endpoint
logic, eligibility criteria, evidence quality, or study-readiness materials.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify study objective, population, intervention or exposure, comparator,
   endpoints, timeline, and data collection plan.
2. Separate protocol design review from medical advice, patient-specific
   recommendations, and regulatory approval decisions.
3. Check whether endpoints, eligibility criteria, sample assumptions, safety
   monitoring, and missing-data handling align with the stated objective.
4. Flag ethics, consent, privacy, adverse-event, and regulatory questions for
   qualified specialist review.
5. Produce a protocol-readiness assessment with evidence gaps and verification
   questions.

## Expected Output

- study design summary
- endpoint and eligibility critique
- bias, safety, and data-quality risks
- ethics and regulatory escalation notes
- protocol-readiness checklist

## Verifier Expectations

- PICO or study-objective alignment check
- endpoint and population consistency check
- safety and ethics escalation check
- medical-advice boundary check

## Failure Handling

If protocol details are missing, provide a study-design question list and avoid
claiming clinical validity.

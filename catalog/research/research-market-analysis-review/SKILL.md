# Research Market Analysis Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when reviewing market research, opportunity sizing, competitor
signals, buyer evidence, or market-entry assumptions.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify market definition, geography, segment, time period, source set, and
   decision the research supports.
2. Separate primary evidence, secondary sources, estimates, assumptions,
   extrapolations, and unknowns.
3. Check TAM, SAM, SOM, growth, competitor, and buyer claims for source lineage
   and method clarity.
4. Flag stale data, mixed definitions, selection bias, and unsupported
   precision.
5. Produce findings as research evidence, not market certainty.

## Expected Output

- market scope and source map
- claim and evidence table
- sizing method risks
- competitor and buyer signal notes
- confidence and refresh recommendations

## Verifier Expectations

- source lineage check
- market definition check
- unsupported precision check
- freshness and bias check

## Failure Handling

If sources conflict or definitions drift, preserve both interpretations and
state the decision risk.

# Research Claude Skills Evidence Review

## When To Use

Use this skill when a backlog candidate concerns grants, dossiers, literature
review, patent review, syllabus planning, NotebookLM-style note workflows,
deep research, source freshness, or evidence synthesis.

## Safe Workflow

1. Identify research question, audience, source requirements, citation style,
   freshness requirement, and artifact.
2. Separate discovery, extraction, synthesis, claim verification, and final
   presentation.
3. Prefer existing research source-check, citation, paper synthesis, market,
   product, and finance research skills when coverage is sufficient.
4. Convert candidates into bounded outputs such as source matrices, evidence
   maps, grant-fit tables, patent landscape notes, or literature extraction
   schemas.
5. Mark unsupported claims, missing primary sources, stale sources, and
   unresolved conflicts explicitly.

## Expected Output

- research question and source criteria
- evidence extraction or citation schema
- freshness and conflict notes
- trusted-skill coverage map
- adoption decision and verification gaps

## Verifier Expectations

- primary-source check
- citation and claim-evidence check
- freshness check when relevant
- candidate-map coverage check

## Failure Handling

If source access is unavailable or citations are insufficient, produce a
research plan and mark synthesis conclusions as unverified.
